Our Healthy Recipes 🍽️

Easy, tasty and nutritious ideas for everyday meals.

🥗 Fresh Garden Salad

A refreshing mix of colorful vegetables.

Approx. 180 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Cucumber
  • Tomato and carrot
  • Lettuce
  • Lemon juice and salt

Preparation: Chop the vegetables, mix them and enjoy fresh.

🥣 Fruit Oatmeal

A comforting breakfast with fiber and fruit.

Approx. 300 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Half a cup of oats
  • Milk or a suitable alternative
  • Banana slices
  • Nuts and cinnamon

Preparation: Cook the oats with milk and add the toppings.

🍓 Berry Smoothie

A fruity drink packed with delicious flavor.

Approx. 200 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Strawberries or mixed berries
  • Half a banana
  • Plain yogurt
  • A little milk

Preparation: Blend all the ingredients until smooth.

🍚 Veggie Rice Bowl

A wholesome meal with grains and vegetables.

Approx. 350 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Cooked rice
  • Carrot and beans
  • Green peas
  • Oil and mild spices

Preparation: Cook the vegetables and mix them with rice.

🥕 Vegetable Soup

A warm and comforting vegetable dish.

Approx. 150 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Carrot
  • Beans and peas
  • Tomato
  • Water and seasoning

Preparation: Simmer the vegetables in water until tender.

🫘 Chickpea Salad

A tasty plant-based source of protein and fiber.

Approx. 250 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Boiled chickpeas
  • Cucumber and tomato
  • Chopped onion
  • Lemon juice and spices

Preparation: Mix everything in a bowl and serve.
